SSC 490-005: AI Workflows & Organizational Intelligence

Fall 2026 • Michigan State University • Jesse Flores & Maya Craft
Project-based course in which student teams build a working AI system for a real business partner — moving from primitives (prompts, embeddings, models) through compositions (retrieval, vector search, multi-modal) into deployment (agents, skills, automated workflows). Students hold their own Claude.ai Team seats and API keys this term.
Semester shape: Tuesday / Thursday, 80 minutes • 28 sessions across 15 weeks • Classes begin Aug 31, 2026 • Last day of classes Dec 11 • Finals Dec 14–18.
⚠ Arc under design. Dates and session numbers below are final and will not move. Sessions 1–8 are titled; decks are built as each week approaches. Later sessions are not yet assigned — the Fall arc is being rebuilt around the pilot’s delivered path, and the partner project shape differs from Spring. The 23 decks carried over from the SSC 493 pilot are listed at the bottom as source material awaiting remap.
