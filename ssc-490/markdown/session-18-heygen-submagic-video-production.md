## Demo: Submagic

<div style="font-size: 20px; line-height: 1.8; margin: 30px 0;">

1. Upload your HeyGen video
2. **Generate B-Rolls** → Magic B-Rolls + Magic Zooms
3. **Remove 30-50%** of generated B-roll (less is more)
4. **Captions** → fix errors → style font/color/animation
5. Sound effects on **2-3 key words max**
6. Export → **1080x1920 (9:16)**

</div>

<div style="background: #dbeafe; padding: 15px; border-radius: 10px; margin: 20px 0; font-size: 18px; text-align: center;">
85% of social video is watched on mute. Captions aren't optional.
</div>

<div style="font-size: 18px; margin: 20px 0;">

**Playbook:** https://www.superwebpros.com/blog/enhance-videos-submagic

</div>

Note:
Use a pre-rendered HeyGen video for this demo — don't wait for a live render. Walk through: upload → processing (1-2 min) → generate B-rolls → show Magic B-Rolls and Magic Zooms → then remove a bunch of them. "Submagic over-generates. Remove 30-50% for a cleaner look." Then captions: auto-generated, fix errors, style them. Position for platform: Reels/TikTok = center or upper-third, Instagram feed = center, LinkedIn = lower-third. Sound effects: 2-3 max, only on the most important words. Preview, then export at 9:16. If time, show switching to 1:1 for Instagram feed. Playbook: superwebpros.com/articles/enhance-videos-submagic

## Production Sprint

<div style="font-size: 18px; line-height: 1.6; margin: 20px 0;">

**Pipeline your work — never wait idle:**

1. Submit script #1 to HeyGen → while rendering...
2. Submit script #2 to HeyGen → while rendering...
3. Script #1 done → download → Submagic → while processing...
4. Script #2 done → download → Submagic...
5. Export → next script

</div>

<div style="font-size: 16px; margin: 20px 0;">

| Team Size | Target: Submitted | Target: Finished |
|-----------|------------------|-----------------|
| 2-3 | 4-5 videos | 2-3 enhanced |
| 4-5 | 6-8 videos | 3-4 enhanced |

</div>

Note:
50 minutes for production. The key message: "You should never be waiting idle. If HeyGen is rendering, submit the next script. If Submagic is processing, review captions on the previous video." Circulating questions: "Which reel script did you pick first? Why?" "What avatar and voice did you choose? Does it match your partner's brand?" "How does the B-roll look? Did you remove the generic stuff?" "Watch this on your phone — can you read the captions?" For teams without accounts: pair with another team or use HeyGen free trial. No one sits idle — if they can't make videos, they refine their reel agent or process more pillars.
