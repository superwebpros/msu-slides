## How Today Goes

<div style="font-size: 23px; line-height: 1.9; margin: 50px auto; max-width: 860px; text-align: left;">

**1.** Learn about the organization

**2.** Ask questions to understand the job we'll be working on

**3.** Back here with about 15 minutes left — questions and concerns

</div>

<div style="font-size: 19px; margin: 30px auto 0 auto; max-width: 860px; color: #334155;">
One room per organization. If your org has more than one team, you're all in there together.
</div>

Note:
Don't run this to the minute. Multi-team rooms: most of the hour you're listening to projects that aren't yours — that's how you learn the organization.
Ask out loud before recording: "We'd like to record this so our notes are accurate — is that okay with you?" If they say no, written notes only.
Don't collect passwords. I set up secure credential sharing later.
Everyone asks questions. One scribe per team owns the capture page. Pick them before you walk in, and sort out who's recording.
